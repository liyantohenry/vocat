VoCat Screen Surfaces

Every surface the device shows on its 360 × 360 round screen, and every page it serves over Wi-Fi, drawn at their real proportions. The battery readout on the clock page is new and proposed; everything else is on the board today.

On the device

Swipe left or right to move between Clock, Media, Desktop and Neurons. The other surfaces take over when they apply.

Battery level proposed

The board has the same battery gauge chip as Espressif's VoCat (a BQ27220 on the shared I²C bus, at 0x55). The stock CyberAI firmware already reads it, so the clock page can show a real percentage and whether the battery is charging.

In a browser

The board runs a small web server: the setup pages on its own network, and the settings page at its address on your Wi-Fi.

How the battery readout would work

Bottom of the clock pageA small battery glyph and the percentage, under the rate ticker. The time and weather stay where they are, and a notice line still takes the ticker's place.
Five steps, not a smooth barThe glyph comes from the font's built-in battery symbols (full, ¾, ½, ¼, empty, plus a charge bolt), so it moves in quarters. The percentage beside it is exact.
Colour only when it mattersGrey above 20 %, amber at 20 % and below, red at 10 % and below with "Charge me". Blue with a bolt while charging, and "Charged" once it's full on USB.
Read every 30 secondsA normal task reads the gauge and hands the result to the screen, the same way the weather does. If the gauge doesn't answer, the readout stays hidden.
